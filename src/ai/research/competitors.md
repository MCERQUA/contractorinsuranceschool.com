# Competitor Landscape — Glulam Manufacturer Insurance

## Specialty Timber Insurance Markets (Carriers/Programs)
- Note: Most standard markets (Hartford, Travelers, CNA) have exclusions or sub-limits for products liability for structural timber manufacturers
- E&S markets (Lloyd's syndicates, specialty domestic E&S) are the primary placement channel for glulam products liability with real limits
- SFPA (Southern Forest Products Association) and NLBMDA have group programs but often GL only

## Agency Competitors
- Most generalist agencies lack specialty timber market relationships
- Few agencies specifically position on glulam/mass timber manufacturer programs
- Adjacent: lumber yard insurance, sawmill insurance, general wood products manufacturing insurance

## CCA Differentiators vs. Generic Markets
1. Products liability that actually covers manufactured glulam members (not carved out)
2. Workers' comp with correct classification for kiln, press, CNC, and crane operators
3. Inland marine from plant door to crane lift (not just transit)
4. Equipment breakdown included — covers internal press/kiln failures
5. E&S market access for manufacturers with prior losses on beam failures
6. Former contractor background = language fluency with plant operators

## Adjacent Markets to Watch
- CLT (cross-laminated timber) manufacturers — similar products liability exposure
- LVL (laminated veneer lumber) manufacturers
- Mass timber architects and structural engineers (E&O adjacent)
- Heavy timber framing contractors (completed-operations adjacent)
