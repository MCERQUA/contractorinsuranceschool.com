# Topical Map — gluelammanufacturerinsurance.com

**Niche:** commercial insurance for glue-laminated timber (glulam) / engineered
wood product / mass-timber **manufacturers** (the plants — not the contractors).
**Own:** the exact phrase "glulam manufacturer insurance" (zero competitors target it).

## Pillar topics (3)
1. **Glulam Manufacturer Insurance** (root pillar / homepage) — the full program:
   every coverage a glulam/EWP plant needs, coordinated into one program.
2. **Mass Timber & Engineered Wood Product Risk** — the product-liability +
   property/equipment story unique to EWP manufacturing (delamination, combustible
   dust, press/kiln breakdown, oversized-beam transit).
3. **Protecting the Production Line** — business interruption + equipment breakdown
   narrative (a down press or kiln fire = idle millions in annual output).
