# Competitor Scan — Glulam / Mass-Timber / Wood-Products Manufacturer Insurance

**Method:** Web search for insurers/brokers writing wood-products, lumber-mill,
sawmill, and mass-timber manufacturing insurance. Compiled 2026-06-17.
Observations directional; live SERPs shift.

## ⭐ Headline finding
**Zero competitors own "glulam manufacturer insurance" as a niche.** They target
generic "wood products," "lumberyards," or "sawmills." No agency has a program,
page, or content specifically for **glue-laminated timber / CLT / mass-timber
manufacturers**. The closest is a Woodruff-Sawyer *article* (via JD Supra) — a
thought-leadership piece, not an offering. **gluelammanufacturerinsurance.com is
an SEO monopoly on the exact phrase.** Build around exact-match manufacturing
vocabulary no one else uses.

## Common patterns
- **CTA:** "Request a Quote" form or "Find an Agent" + phone. Rarely real online
  quoting (Insureon/Novum are the exceptions).
- **Trust:** years in business, A.M. Best ratings, trade-association memberships,
  named expert, client testimonials, claims-satisfaction metric.
- **Content:** mostly shallow (0–3 posts). Best: Travelers (corporate library), HUB
  (kiln/safety posts), Horton (3+ posts).
- **Vocabulary:** generic — "wood products," "lumber," "sawmills." Best-in-class
  specificity: thermoscan, stock through-put, products-completed operations.

## Gaps & opportunities (what they MISS — our wedge)
1. **No one owns glulam/mass-timber manufacturing** as a niche. Own the exact phrase.
2. **Missing glulam vocabulary:** finger-jointing, face-bonding, planing, moisture
   content & standards (**ANSI A190.1**, ANSI 117, CSA O177, PRG 320 for CLT),
   **delamination** risk, **combustible dust / NFPA 664**, hydraulic **and
   radio-frequency (RF) curing press** breakdown, adhesive/formaldehyde emissions
   liability, QC documentation for underwriters, **MTIAP** (Mass Timber Insurance
   Action Plan), stock-throughput timber→plant→site.
3. **Products-completed ops / E&O for design guidance** — manufacturers who advise on
   connection details, span tables, install methods need Professional Liability.
   Only Travelers mentions Manufacturers E&O; none tie it to mass timber.
4. **Stock through-put / transit for oversized EWP** — glulam beams run 60–100+ ft;
   specialized flatbed, permits, escorts, loading/unloading liability. Only ECM names
   stock through-put.
5. **Business interruption + equipment breakdown for kiln + press lines** — a press
   failure or kiln fire can idle $10M+ of annual production. No unified view.
6. **Manufacturer product-liability depth:** structural failure, delamination, fire
   performance (charring rates/fire ratings), water/mold in transit/storage, code
   compliance (IBC, ICC-ES evaluation reports). Carriers are wary — manufacturers must
   "tell their story" to underwriters (per Woodruff-Sawyer).
7. **No deep content library** for engineered-wood insurance. Build 10–15 articles.
8. **No manufacturing-specific trust signals** — NFPA 664 / OSHA 1910.263 guidance,
   carrier-appetite info, real handled-claim case studies.

## Trust signals & CTAs to mirror/beat
- **Trust:** 50-state license (CCA's true differentiator), carrier-partner ratings,
  named glulam specialist, manufacturer testimonials, trade associations (APA,
  WoodWorks, SLMA), A.M. Best carrier ratings, risk-control resources, claims metric.
- **CTAs:** glulam-specific quote form (products, # presses, kiln capacity, revenue,
  employees, states), "talk to a glulam specialist" phone, downloadable glulam
  insurance guide lead-magnet, free risk-assessment, "compare your current coverage."
